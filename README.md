## About the project

As a dancer with a background in modern K-pop and J-pop idol choreography, I am investigating how technology can support independent dance practice.

The project focuses on three connected needs:

1. **Seeing the execution** - helping dancers observe their movements without relying on installed mirrors.
2. **Identifying discrepancies** - distinguishing meaningful movement mistakes from acceptable stylistic variation.
3. **Understanding corrections** - providing clear adjustments that dancers can understand and practise.

The proposed direction is a VR practice system combining live avatar observation, reference viewing, playback controls, and targeted feedback.

## Current progress

During Semester 1, I surveyed 35 dancers, reviewed existing studies, and investigated Sound Game Training, Sway Formations, and lighthouse-based full-body tracking in VR.

Semester 2 will focus on developing the functional prototype, defining movement-assessment criteria with dance teachers and item ICs, and evaluating whether dancers can understand and apply the generated feedback.
